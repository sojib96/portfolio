About

I started as a backend engineer, building Django APIs and debugging database queries. Somewhere along the way I realized the part of the work I cared about most was the question “how do we know this actually works?” That curiosity pulled me into quality assurance — and it's where I've stayed.

Over the last two years I've built automation frameworks from scratch for healthcare platforms, designed regression suites that guard multi-module HR systems, and learned that great testing isn't about catching every bug — it's about giving teams the confidence to ship without fear. I work across the full stack of quality: test architecture, automated suites, API validation, and cross-functional collaboration inside Agile workflows.

When I'm not designing test infrastructure, I publish research on deep learning, solve problems on LeetCode, and explore open-source automation tooling.
