About

I started as a backend engineer. Somewhere between building APIs and debugging database queries, I realized the part of the job I cared most about was the question "how do we know this actually works?" That shift in perspective took me into quality assurance — and I haven't looked back.

Over the last two years I've built automation frameworks from scratch for healthcare platforms, designed regression suites that guard multi-module HR systems, and learned that great testing isn't about catching every bug — it's about creating the confidence that lets teams move fast without fear. I work across the full stack of quality: designing test architecture, writing automated suites, validating APIs, and collaborating with developers inside Agile workflows.

When I'm not breaking things deliberately, I publish research on deep learning, solve problems on LeetCode, and explore open-source test automation tooling.
