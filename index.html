About

My path into quality assurance started on the development side. While interning as a backend engineer, I found myself more drawn to the question of how do we know this works? than the code itself. That curiosity led me to SQA, where I've spent the last two years building test infrastructure for healthcare platforms, fintech tools, and e-commerce systems across Dhaka's tech scene.

I specialize in designing automation frameworks from scratch — architecting test suites that teams can trust and build on. Whether it's a Playwright pipeline catching regressions before deployment or a Selenium suite integrated into CI/CD, my focus is always on creating testing strategies that scale with the product. I work best at the intersection of development and QA, translating complex business logic into reliable, repeatable test coverage.

Outside of work, I publish academic research on machine learning applications, sharpen my problem-solving skills on LeetCode, and contribute to open-source test automation projects. I believe the best engineers are the ones who never stop being curious about how things could break — and how to prevent it.
